The house

The river runs from the lake to the Caribbean through a gorge of limestone and forest, and the houses along it are reached the way everything here is reached: by boat. Ki’ sits on its own stretch of bank, six rooms under one thatched roof, with the dock as its front door.

It was built for a large family and it works best full. The kitchen is open, the table is long, and the hammocks along the water take the afternoon.

The gorge begins ten minutes downstream. Lívingston and the sea are an hour beyond it.

Inside

  • The roofSix rooms under one thatch, each with its own bath
  • The dockThe front door, with kayaks tied up beside it
  • The tableAn open kitchen and a table for fourteen
  • The hammocksAlong the water, in the shade, for the afternoons
  • The airScreens on every window, fans in every room

Around the house

  • Downriver, under the canopyThe gorge at the hour the herons move, and the Caribbean at the end of it.
  • The fort at San FelipeA small Spanish castle at the mouth of the lake, best at the end of the day.
  • A Garifuna kitchen in LívingstonTapado, coconut and fish, cooked by the family that taught us the town.

Practical

  • ArrivalFive hours from the airport by road, or forty minutes by air to Puerto Barrios, then the boat. We arrange all of it.
  • The house teamA cook, a housekeeper and a boatman, every day.
  • HoursIn from two in the afternoon, out by eleven.
  • ChildrenWelcome at any age. Life jackets in every size are in the house.

Not here

  • No air conditioning in the common rooms. The bedrooms have it.
  • No road. Everything, and everyone, arrives by water.
  • No mobile signal at the dock. The house has Wi-Fi.
