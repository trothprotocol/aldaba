The house

Antigua is a grid of cobbled streets inside a ring of volcanoes, and most of its houses hide behind a wall. Ja’ is one of them. From the street there is a door and a bell; inside, a garden runs the length of the plot with Agua standing at the end of it.

The house was built for a family that likes to cook and eat outside. The kitchen opens onto the garden, the table seats ten, and the four bedrooms sit around a courtyard where the mornings are quiet enough to hear the fountain.

The plaza is eight minutes on foot. The car is inside the walls, which matters more in Antigua than anywhere else.
