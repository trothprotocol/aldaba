The house

Guatemala City is cut through by ravines, and the forest that fills them is older than the city. Tinamit is built on the lip of one: two floors of glass and concrete held in the trees, with the canopy at eye level and the city out of sight.

It is the house we use for the first and last night of a journey, and for anyone who has work to do. The desk faces the forest, the connection is fast, and the museums and restaurants of the city are fifteen minutes down the hill.

Two bedrooms, so it suits a couple or a small family. Larger groups go to Antigua, which is an hour away.
