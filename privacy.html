Privacy.

Short, because there is little to say.

This site has no accounts, no payments and no analytics, and it sets no cookies.

What the site keeps: if you use Sign in, your name and email are stored in your own browser, on this device, and nowhere else. The same goes for your language and currency choice. Clearing your browser data removes them, and so does Forget me in the Sign in window.

What we receive: every form on this site writes an email in your own mail client, and nothing is sent until you send it. When you do, we receive what you wrote, and we keep it for as long as we are arranging, or might arrange, a stay for you.

What we do with it: we reply. We do not sell, share or rent your details, and we do not add you to a list you did not ask to join.

The site is served by Vercel, which keeps the technical logs any web server keeps.

Ask us to show you, correct or delete anything we hold about you, at any time.

Questions about any of this: hola@ladantajourneys.com.
