What we build around the house.

A house is where you sleep. The days are the reason you came. Everything below is arranged by us, priced plainly, and never bundled into the house.

Antigua

Coffee before the city wakes

The finca is twenty minutes above town, on the slope. You arrive in the dark and the picking starts as the light does. By eight there is a table set under the trees, and the coffee in the cup was on the branch an hour before.

We arrange the car, the finca and the table. You bring an appetite and something warm for the first hour.

Lake Atitlán

The lake while it is still glass

The wind arrives at midday, every day, so the morning belongs to the water. A boat at first light, a swim off the rocks, and a slow crossing to Santiago, where a Tz’utujil kitchen has lunch waiting.

Back before the wind gets up, with the afternoon for nothing at all.

Río Dulce

Downriver, under the canopy

The river narrows into a gorge, the walls go up three hundred feet and the herons move along them in the early light. At the far end the water opens into the Caribbean at Lívingston.

A day on the water with someone who knows every bend, lunch on the way, and the boat back before dark.
