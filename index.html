Concierge

Everything else, quietly arranged.

Ask for anything. The cars, the drivers and the days out belong to companies we work with directly, which is why we can promise them rather than pass on a number.

  • The arrival

    A driver holding your name however late the flight lands, and the house open and lit when you reach it.

  • In the kitchen

    The house stocked before you arrive, to your own list, down to the coffee you drink at home.

  • At the table

    A chef for one night or for every night, cooking whatever the market had that morning, at the hour you feel like eating.

  • For the body

    A massage where you are sitting, a temazcal heated at dusk, and a teacher who comes to the house at six if you want one.

  • Days out

    Private boats, private guides, and the volcano to yourselves an hour before anybody else is on it.

  • The small things

    Flowers cut that morning, a cake for the birthday you did not mention, and one number that is answered at any hour of the night.
