Property owners

The Ladanta Standard.

What we promise, and how we keep it. Most rentals hand you a key and a phone number. We hand you a person, a house we know by heart, and a country arranged around your stay.

Very few houses

We do not list every house that asks. Every house on the list is one we have walked through ourselves, room by room, before a guest ever sees it.

We go back. When a house stops meeting the standard, it leaves the list, whatever it earned us. A short list we can stand behind is worth more than a long one we cannot.

One person, all the way through

From your first message to your last morning, the same person answers. They know the house, the road to it, and what you asked for three weeks ago.

Nothing is passed between departments, because there are none. You never explain yourself twice.

Everything around the house

The car from the airport, the cook for the evening, the boat at first light, the guide who has walked the trail since he was a boy, the table at the place that does not take bookings. Arranged before you land, and adjusted quietly while you are here.

Priced plainly. You see what each thing costs before you say yes, and there is nothing added at the end.

A stay should be worth the trip.

The companies behind us

Ladanta is a new name from an old group. The same companies have been at this for generations, and between them they run the cars, the drivers, the guides and the journeys.

It is why we can say a car will be at the door at midnight and mean it. It is our car, and our driver.

Six decades of receiving travellers in this country.

What makes it different

A booking site knows the listing. A local agency knows the town. We know the house, the town, the road between them and the people on it, and we own the part of the trip most stays leave to chance.

That is the standard: fewer houses, one person, and everything around the stay done properly.
