Property owners

We wanted the kind of stay we could not find here. So we built the company that would arrange it.

Ladanta represents a small number of private homes in Guatemala and arranges everything around a stay in them.

The idea

Guatemala has extraordinary private homes. What it has not had is one company that will find you the right one and then take care of everything around it: the car from the airport, the cook, the boat, the guide, the table at the place that does not take bookings.

That is the whole of what we do. We take on very few homes, we know each of them personally, and one person sees a stay through from the first message to the last morning.

The name

La Danta is a pyramid at El Mirador, deep in the Petén forest, raised by the Maya two thousand years ago and still among the largest structures on earth by volume. The danta is also the tapir, the largest animal that walks this isthmus, found from Guatemala to Panama.

We took the name for both: something built to last, and something that belongs to the whole region.

The people behind it

Ladanta is a new name from an old group. For six decades the same companies have been receiving travelers in this country: Carma Group, Hertz Guatemala, Trasla, Maya Trails and Trip18 between them run the cars, the drivers, the guides and the journeys.

It is why we can say a car will be at the door at midnight and mean it. It is our car, and our driver.

A stay should be worth the trip.
